STYLE: 1930s rubber hose cartoon, hand-inked cel animation look, original characters.
Thick uniform outline in dark plum #3D3346 on every shape, clean confident lines, no sketchy strokes.
Flat cel colors with at most one soft shade tone per color, no gradients on characters, no 3D rendering, no glossy plastic look; one small white highlight dot is allowed.
Characters: bendy rubber hose arms and legs with no elbows or knees, white four-finger cartoon gloves, big round shoes, pie-cut eyes (black ovals with a small wedge cut out), simple wide mouth, big head-to-body ratio.
Character colors are saturated: red #D2463C, blue #3E6DB5, yellow #F2C14E, cream #F4E6C8, glove white #FBF6EA.
Backgrounds and large props look like faded watercolor on paper in beige #CFAC8D, sand #D7BE97, dusty navy #4E546E, muted olive #8A9A6B, warm grey #907F77, and are LESS saturated than the characters.
Soft light from the top-left. No cast shadow on the ground. No text, letters or numbers anywhere.
Do not copy any existing cartoon or game character, logo, or scene.

Each item isolated on a flat solid #FF00FF magenta background (or transparent), full figure visible, nothing cropped, wide empty margin around every item, no ground line, no floor, no frame, no border, no shadow under the item.
Characters face the viewer at a slight high 3/4 angle unless a direction is given. "Side" always means facing RIGHT.

Exactly TWO separate button sprites in a two-row sheet, centered one above the other, same outer dimensions and silhouette, each approximately 4:1 width to height. Top: red START button with small winding-key pictogram. Bottom: cream HELP button with small open-book pictogram. Icon occupies only leftmost 18 percent; preserve 70 percent of the button face as plain empty area for code-rendered Korean text. No letters, numbers, labels or pseudo-writing. Clean chunky hand-inked lines, little solid darker base lip to suggest pressability but NO cast shadow. No ornamental protrusions that disrupt the rounded rectangular hit area. Pure #FF00FF background, generous margin and gap; exactly two objects.

one wide rounded rectangular cartoon start button, muted red face, brass and cream double rim, thick dark plum outline, a small brass butterfly winding key pictogram on the left, broad empty center-right for a Korean label added by code, no lettering, no cast shadow

one wide rounded rectangular cartoon help button, cream face, brass and dark plum double rim, thick dark plum outline, a small open book pictogram with blank pages on the left, broad empty center-right for a Korean label added by code, no lettering, no cast shadow

References: style_anchor.png is the primary project drawing style and palette. ref_02_character.jpg is technique only; do not copy its character, title or lettering. Other supplied project artwork is for continuity.

At least 300 pixels of EMPTY MAGENTA between the two buttons. Each is a simple rounded rectangle with short brass corners, NO FAN ORNAMENTS above or below, NO flourishes sticking out, no connecting decorations. They must not touch. Preserve isolated game-button design, NOT long intertitle-card banners.
